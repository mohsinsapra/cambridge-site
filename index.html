Setup

Connected in under a minute

  1. 01

    Open CamBridge Camera on iPhone

    Launch the app — it puts your iPhone's camera on the local network, ready to be found.

  2. 02

    Open CamBridge Studio on Mac

    Launch the Mac app on the same Wi‑Fi network and your iPhone shows up automatically.

  3. 03

    Connected instantly over Wi‑Fi

    The live feed appears on your Mac in seconds, with full remote control ready to go.

Built in Swift. No accounts. No cloud.

CamBridge Camera and CamBridge Studio are native Swift apps that talk to each other directly over your local Wi‑Fi network. There's no sign‑up, no server in the middle, and your video never leaves your network. What's on your camera stays between your iPhone and your Mac.
