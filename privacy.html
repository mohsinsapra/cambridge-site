Privacy Policy

Nothing to collect, because nothing leaves your network.

CamBridge Camera (iPhone) and CamBridge Studio (Mac) are built to work entirely on your local network, between your own devices. This page explains exactly what that means.

We collect nothing

CamBridge does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data, usage data, or analytics. There is no cloud backend, no server in the middle, and no data pipeline of any kind.

Video and control data stay on your local network

The video feed and every control signal — focus, zoom, exposure, torch, and the rest — travel directly over Wi‑Fi between your iPhone and your Mac. This connection never leaves your local network and never touches the internet.

Recordings stay on your devices

Anything you capture or record is saved locally on the device that captured it — your iPhone or your Mac. Recordings are never uploaded anywhere by CamBridge.

No accounts, no analytics, no third parties

There's no sign‑up and no account of any kind. CamBridge does not use analytics, crash‑reporting, advertising, or any other third‑party SDK. No data is shared with anyone, because none is collected in the first place.
